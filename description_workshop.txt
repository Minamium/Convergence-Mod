[h1]Convergence (Development Build)[/h1]
A public playtest mod for Terraria / tModLoader, designed around Calamity endgame equipment.

[h2]Content[/h2]
[b]Requiem of the Hollow Doll — initial prototype complete.[/b]
Fight [b]Lacrimosa — The Bound Heart[/b], a tragic suspended Doll, through multiple phases and a final survival sequence. Coordinate Stack and Spread, revive teammates, and earn a Curtainfall Treasure Box. Each box contains one of five class weapons at equal odds. Collect all five to craft The Unbroken Promise, a ten-slot Doll companion who walks, rides a broom and fights beside you. She is a minion, not a Raid participant.

[b]Ghost Samurai — in development.[/b]
An independent summoned boss with its own attacks and normal player death. Rewards, balance and lifecycle work are unfinished. It is not another completed Raid.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Calamity Mod 2.2.4 and Luminance 1.0.14, plus their required dependencies. Tested baseline: tModLoader 2026.07.3.0 / Terraria 1.4.4.9.
[*]Recommended: 2–4 players, Calamity endgame gear, and a world with Exo Mechs and Supreme Calamitas defeated.
[*]Solo activation is available for playtesting, but the Raid has not been rebalanced for solo. Multiplayer feedback is especially welcome.
[*]Every client and server must use the same Convergence build.
[/list]

[h2]Starting the Raid[/h2]
[list]
[*]Foundation Core, Theater Doll and Resuscitation Kit do not yet have normal recipes. Obtain them using an item browser/spawner such as Cheat Sheet; it is a setup aid, not a required dependency.
[*]Place Foundation Core on solid ground with ample clear space around and above it.
[*]Hold Theater Doll and click the pedestal. Preparation deploys for the connected party; each player then marks Ready.
[*]Gather in the marked circle for Stack. Separate so player circles do not overlap for Spread. A correctly completed mechanic deals no damage.
[*]Use Resuscitation Kit near a Downed ally for instant revival. It is reusable, without shared tokens; the recipient has a 60-second revival lockout. All participants Down means defeat.
[/list]

[h2]Development build / known limitations[/h2]
Back up worlds and characters; use test saves. Balance, compatibility, presentation and future save compatibility are not guaranteed.
A known Ghost Samurai world-unload exception can occur even if that boss was not summoned. Exit/re-entry and multiplayer edge cases still need work. Solo victory is not full multiplayer validation.
Contains strong flashes, screen shake and sound. Reduced-effects options are available.

[h2]日本語[/h2]
Dollレイド「Requiem of the Hollow Doll」は初期プロトタイプ完成、Ghost Samurai（幽鬼武者）は引き続き開発中です。まだ完成版ではないため、感想・不具合報告をお待ちしています。
ソロでも起動できますが、可能なら2～4人のマルチでのテストをお願いします。Calamity最終装備、Exo Mechs・Supreme Calamitas撃破済みワールドを想定しています。全員のModバージョンをそろえ、ワールド・キャラクターをバックアップしてください。

Ghost Samuraiを呼ばなくてもワールド終了時の例外が起こる既知問題があります。強い発光・揺れ・効果音にもご注意ください。詳しい既知問題・検証範囲はGitHubのリリースノートに記載しています。

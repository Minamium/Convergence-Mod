## Murasama motion reference — 2026-09-17

Question: how does the named weapon separate continuous wielding, slash beats and stronger-hit presentation? Inspected the official public repository at the same pinned commit above: [Murasama](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/Items/Weapons/Melee/Murasama.cs), [MurasamaSlash](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/Projectiles/Melee/MurasamaSlash.cs), [build manifest](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/build.txt) and [license](https://github.com/CalamityTeam/CalamityModPublic/blob/1a8cebd27ec5615316b78f71973446b5528d2b78/LICENSE.md), accessed September17. The manifest declares2.2.2, so these observations are not a verified description of installed2.2.4.

Observed: `AI` cycles a14-frame slash sheet every3 updates, with three specific slash beats; `PreDraw` selects that frame around a hand-relative projectile. Aim updates at slash beats. The heavy beat distinguishes sound, sparks and hit response. No `oldPos`/`oldRot` trail cache or easing-based blade rotation was found in these two files; much of the trajectory is authored in the animation. Adopt the readable three-beat loop and stronger final-cut presentation, not its channel/projectile/hit implementation or artwork.

Oboro independently uses a shared piecewise Hermite rotation curve with connected velocities and a heavy follow-through that meets the next stance modulo one turn. A separate bounded client pose history gives violet blade echoes and moonlit edge ribbons. Existing server-owned timing, swept collision and per-enemy hit cap remain authoritative; visual entry blending ends before damage. New checks cover live alignment, curve bounds, seams and transient history lifecycle. Actual SP/MP readability and equipped DPS comparison remain playtest questions, not source-research conclusions.
